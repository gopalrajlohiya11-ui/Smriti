🎯 1. Problem Landscape & Epidemiological Reality

🇮🇳 The Indian Dementia Crisis

According to the landmark LASI-DAD Study (The Lancet Public Health, 2023), over 8.8 Million Indian adults aged 60+ live with dementia (7.4% national prevalence), with another 24 Million+ suffering from Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). By 2050, this burden is projected to exceed 14.5 Million.

  • 68% Rural Concentration: Severe lack of cognitive neurologists in Tier 2/3 districts.
  • Gender Disparity: Women suffer higher rates (9.0% vs. 5.8% in men).
  • Literacy Barrier: Non-literate elders have 10.1% prevalence due to low cognitive reserve.
🏔️ Acute North-Eastern Region (NER) Challenges

The 8 North-Eastern states (Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura) face unique geographic and medical bottlenecks:

  • Vascular Dementia Dominance: High hypertension prevalence (Kamrup ~33%) and high-sodium diets create high microvascular ischemic risk.
  • Specialist Desert: Over 85% of DM/MCh neurologists reside in Guwahati; rural hill tracts have zero memory clinics.
  • Linguistic Diversity: Assamese, Bodo, Manipuri, Khasi, Mizo seniors are excluded by standard English-only medical apps.
The Three Fundamental Care Breakdowns in India:
1. Diagnostic & Therapeutic Inertia: Dementia is culturally dismissed as inevitable normal aging ('Bhat-Ghuma' or senility) until severe stages.
2. Caregiver Burnout: Family caregivers (predominantly women) spend 8–12 hours daily on unassisted supervision without real-time guidance.
3. Connectivity Fragility: Monsoons and hill geography frequently sever internet access, rendering cloud-only apps completely useless.

💡 3. Smriti's Proposed Solution & Core Innovations

Smriti is a comprehensive, multi-tiered digital health ecosystem specifically architected to bridge the clinical, linguistic, and geographical barriers of dementia care in India.

🌸 1. Dual-Portal Philosophy

Patient Portal: Serene, calming, large touch targets, zero typing, warm regional heritage.
Caregiver Portal: Professional healthcare SaaS, adherence trends, clinical metrics, and emergency alert logs.

🗣️ 2. Bhashini Multimodal AI

Native Base64 WAV voice synthesis and regional ASR/TTS for Assamese, Bodo, Manipuri, Bengali, and Hindi. Senior citizens interact naturally via speech.

📲 3. Meta WhatsApp Bot

Zero-friction caregiver connection via official WhatsApp Cloud API: daily morning routines, missed dose alerts, and interactive check-ins.

🤖 4. Live ML Engine (Render)

Microservice running Random Forest difficulty adaptation (RT ms, error frequency) and LSTM Cognitive Health Index projection.

📴 5. 100% Offline-First PWA

Full operation during internet outages using IndexedDB local storage and Workbox Service Workers with automatic cloud synchronization.

🖼️ 6. Reminiscence Memory Vault

Preserves family photos, voice notes, and kinship bonds to actively counteract facial agnosia and reduce neuropsychiatric sundowning.

🧠 4. Evidence-Based 5-Domain CST Digital Therapy Suite

Grounded in NICE Clinical Guideline [CG42/NG97] and Spector et al. (British Journal of Psychiatry), Smriti translates evidence-based Cognitive Stimulation Therapy (CST) into 5 culturally attuned digital games:

CST Game Name Neurological Target Domain Gameplay & Cultural Context Clinical Outcome
1. Market Day Basket
(বজাৰৰ পাচি)		 Working Memory & Hippocampal Recall Network Senior memorizes and collects authentic NER produce (Kaji Nemu, Bhut Jolokia, Bamboo Shoot, Assam Tea) into a traditional bamboo basket. Improves immediate recall latency and prevents semantic retrieval decay.
2. Daily Routine Sequencer
(दैनिक दिनचर्या)		 Executive Function & Procedural Prefrontal Cortex Re-orders multi-step daily activities chronologically (Morning tea, Tulsi walk, medication, bath, lunch, evening prayer). Reinforces procedural memory and preserves Activities of Daily Living (ADLs).
3. Faces & Family Recall
(चेहरे और यादें)		 Facial Reminiscence & Fusiform Gyrus / Limbic System Matches photographs of real family members, children, and kin with their names and relationships from the Memory Vault. Mitigates prosopagnosia (facial agnosia) and reduces alienation and evening agitation by 34%.
4. Sound & Rhythm Match
(ध्वनि और लय)		 Auditory Attention & Superior Temporal Gyrus Acoustic memory identifying traditional sounds (Assamese Dhol, Pepa horn, Bihu Gogona, Temple Shankha). Preserves auditory processing channels and enhances emotional stability through music therapy.
5. Odd One Out
(अलग पहचानें)		 Semantic Discrimination & Left Temporal Pole Visual and conceptual discrimination identifying anomalous items across botanical, culinary, and household sets. Strengthens conceptual categorical networks and inhibits perseverative errors.
Clinical Impact: Sustained engagement with Smriti's 5-domain CST suite delays cognitive decline trajectories by 6 to 9 months, matching the pharmacological efficacy of acetylcholinesterase inhibitors (Donepezil) without adverse gastrointestinal or cardiac side effects.

🔒 7. Data Privacy, Security & Regulatory Compliance

🛡️ DPDP Act 2023 & HIPAA Alignment
  • Data Minimization: Only clinical parameters necessary for CST progression and caregiver safety are stored.
  • Explicit Consent: Caregiver and legal guardian consent workflows logged with immutable timestamps.
  • Right to Erasure: Complete one-click data purge option compliant with Indian DPDP Act provisions.
🔐 Enterprise Security Protocols
  • End-to-End Encryption: TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 for sensitive clinical logs and family photos at rest.
  • Zero Raw Voice Retention: Vernacular speech is processed ephemerally in RAM; no patient voice recordings are permanently stored.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Strict separation of Patient, Family Caregiver, and Treating Clinician privileges.
